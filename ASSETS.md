# Asset provenance / Provenienza degli asset

The tournament catalog adds no third-party images, card scans or fonts.
Its decoration is authored CSS; base-printing references are card names and
quantities. / Il catalogo dei mazzi non aggiunge immagini o font di terzi.
Le decorazioni sono CSS; le liste identificano nomi e quantità delle carte.

The upstream card database references remote Lorcast card-image URLs.
Remote art is not downloaded or redistributed by the new catalog.
Its owners retain the artwork and card-text rights. Review permission and
provenance before repackaging these upstream assets or data.

Decorative orbs, gradients, bars and panels are CSS. Emoji are Unicode
characters rendered by the visitor's system; platform emoji artwork is not
bundled. System fonts are not redistributed.

For future additions, record creator, original URL, explicit license and
modifications. A disclaimer does not authorize reuse of protected artwork.
