Evasive Tempo · CASJ

Emerald / Steel

16 / 78 · Randolph Rosemont 5K · 2026-11-01 · CASJ
Original list and reported finish

Game plan

Quest with Evasive characters and use removal and temporary disruption to protect the race. This is the first of the three lists proposed in the shared analysis.

Mulligan

Look for a one-cost and a two-cost character; keep Vixey with Tod when the hand already has enough ink. Return expensive pieces if they stop early development.

Early turns

Develop Tod/Pegasus, then Vixey or another two-drop. Alternate quests and control on turns 3–4. Elinor rewards a board with at least three exerted characters; do not exert everyone automatically.

Fallback and matchups

If you lose the board, contain opposing lore threats with Angel and Strength, then rebuild cheaply. Time Toulouse against Circle and Songs: its action restriction lasts until the start of your next turn.

Detectives Hyperia · Claude Aubé

Amber / Sapphire

8 / 78 · Randolph Rosemont 5K · 2026-11-01 · Claude Aubé
Original list and reported finish

60 cards47/60 inkable24/60 from HyperiaCore · 2026-11-04

Game plan

Play multiple characters in a turn to trigger Detective synergies. Try this candidate early if your collection contains many Hyperia cards.

Mulligan

Look for Higgins or Judy – Helpful Officer, Judy – Day Camp Instructor, a second cheap character and ink for later turns.

Early turns

At three ink you can play a one-drop followed by Day Camp Instructor. Order matters: your second character can trigger ramp and prepare Nick turns. Read each condition; sharing a classification is not sufficient.

Fallback and matchups

If sequencing fails, rebuild resources with Judy – Uncovering Clues and use conditional removal to recover the board. Check target and strength requirements before committing.

Olaf Actions · ClayByte

Amber / Ruby

16 / 49 · Top Choice Gaming Triple Case · 2026-11-01 · ClayByte
Original list and reported finish

60 cards47/60 inkable15/60 from HyperiaCore · 2026-11-04

Game plan

Use actions and items to develop the discard pile and reduce Olaf’s cost. This is a more technical plan with only 16 characters: controlling the board does not itself win the game.

Mulligan

Look for Rivera Family Photo, a cheap action and Liquidator or Jebidiah. Do not keep Olaf at any cost without a functional early hand.

Early turns

Develop resources and survive first. Count action cards in the discard before paying for Olaf; songs are actions. Six discarded actions reduce the printed cost of 9 to 3, before other modifiers.

Fallback and matchups

Defend first against fast aggro. Olaf can slow opposing lore in longer games, but you still need to generate your own points. Learn how to use the Land of the Dead location.

Coco Songs · Hyperia

Amber / Ruby

Proposta tematica / Themed proposal · 2026-10-15 · Brennan DeCandio / Ravensburger
Original list and reported finish

60 cards46/60 inkable33/60 from HyperiaCore · 2026-11-04

Game plan

Ravensburger’s themed starting point, with 33 Hyperia cards out of 60. Treat it as a testing project, not a proven tournament winner or a measured win rate.

Mulligan

Look for a cheap Héctor/Miguel/Pepita base, Angel or Guitar and inkable cards. A hand full of Shift characters without bases is risky.

Early turns

Develop Singers, then use Akood or Meilin for resources. Shift Héctor onto a legal base to develop songs and discard synergies. Evaluate larger Pepita and recursion in the midgame.

Fallback and matchups

Guitar can make an opposing character eligible for Ernesto; Ward prevents choosing a protected opposing target. If Singers are removed, hard-cast songs when needed and rebuild instead of waiting for a perfect combo.

Steelsong: sing or quest?

The shared Amber/Steel plan develops cheap Singers, uses songs for board control and quests with other characters. A character exerted to sing normally cannot also quest in the same turn; a newly played character normally waits until the next turn. Check whether you can reach 20 first, then whether you must stop the opponent winning.

Look for Stitch/Miguel followed by Angel – Siren Singer or Ursula – Vanessa. A dry Singer can sing Akood to develop Aurora or Meilin. Lilo & Stitch can use a dry Stitch base. Dale changes challenge damage: check the card text and board conditions before following the line.

Suggested changes from the chat: −2 This Growing Pressure, +2 The Horseman Strikes!; −1 Bambi – Ethereal Fawn, +1 Ariel – Ethereal Voice. These are three substitutions, not four. The original list was in an image unavailable in the shared page; missing quantities are not reconstructed. This is a primer, not a sixth complete deck list.

Practice one decision at a time

  1. Try Evasive and Detectives first: at least five best-of-three matches each, alternating the starting player. Then try Olaf if its sequencing feels natural.
  2. Record matchup, starting player, turn-5 lore, ink stalls and reason for losing. Measure how quickly you resume questing after a board clear.
  3. Change few cards at once: CASJ replaces one Gaetan with one Lenny compared with Bangkok. One tournament finish does not prove which individual substitution is better.
  4. Against Circle and Songs, time Toulouse and retain resources. Defend first against aggro; choose opposing lore threats carefully in the Evasive mirror. These are testing plans, not measured matchup win rates.

Replacement draws exclude the original seven. This fixed comparison heuristic is not tailored to every archetype.
