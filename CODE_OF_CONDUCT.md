# Community conduct / Condotta della community

Be respectful and constructive. Discuss the work, not the person's worth.
No harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam or publication of
private information. Respect creators' rights and distinguish sourced
facts from opinions.

Rispetta le persone e formula critiche costruttive sul lavoro. Non sono
ammessi molestie, discriminazioni, minacce, spam o diffusione di dati
privati. Rispetta i diritti degli autori e distingui fatti e opinioni.

Report misconduct to repository maintainers using an available private
GitHub channel; if none is available, request a private contact without
posting sensitive details. Maintainers may remove content or restrict
participation according to severity, considering context and good-faith
corrections. Do not publish reports containing another person's private data.
