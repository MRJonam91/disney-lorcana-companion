# Disney Lorcana Cards & Rarities Guide (DE)

Complete guide to card rarity tiers, pull rates, foil variations, and collecting standards.

---

## 1. Rarity Hierarchy

| Symbol | Rarity | Color & Icon | Pack Frequency |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ⚪ | **Common** | Grey Circle | 6 cards per booster pack |
| 📖 | **Uncommon** | White Book | 3 cards per booster pack |
| 🔺 | **Rare** | Bronze Triangle | 2 Rare or higher slots |
| 🔷 | **Super Rare** | Silver Diamond | ~1 in 3-4 packs |
| ⬟ | **Legendary** | Gold Pentagon | ~1 in 6-8 packs (~3-5 per display box) |
| ✨ | **Enchanted** | Rainbow Hexagon | ~1 in 72-96 packs (~1 in 3-4 boxes) |
| 🏆 | **Promo / D23** | Special Foil Crest | Conventions, Tournaments, League Play |

---

## 2. Booster Pack Structure (12 Cards)
- 6 Commons
- 3 Uncommons
- 2 Slots: Rare, Super Rare, or Legendary
- 1 Guaranteed Foil card (any rarity, from Common to Enchanted)

---

## 3. Foil Treatments & Enchanteds
- **Cold Foil**: High-detail localized metallic shine on characters and backgrounds.
- **Enchanted Cards**: Borderless alternative artwork with custom holofoil foiling patterns and secret set numbers.
- **Preservation**: Double sleeving (KMC Perfect Size + Matte outer sleeves) recommended for all foil and high-rarity cards.
