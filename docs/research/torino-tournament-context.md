## Regole / Rules

The [official resources page](https://disneylorcana.com/en/resources/) links
the [Tournament Rules effective 14 July 2026](https://files.disneylorcana.com/Tournament-Rules-7.14.2026_Update_EN.pdf).
Relevant sections:

- **3.2:** 33–64 starting players: six Swiss rounds and Top 8 are advised.
  This does not confirm Torino's structure.
- **3.3:** organizers choose Entry-level/Competitive match structures;
  Premier uses Bo3. Its points are win 3, draw 1, loss 0.
- **3.4:** at time, finish turn zero, then five total additional turns.
  An unfinished game is drawn; Swiss match outcome depends on game wins.
- **6.1:** Swiss rounds have a 50-minute minimum; 60 is the recommended maximum.

Le indicazioni generali non confermano turni, durata o taglio di Torino.
I cinque turni supplementari sono **turni complessivi**, non cinque a giocatore.
Nessun piazzamento è garantito: contano risultati, altri concorrenti e spareggi.
Prepararsi a sei turni è uno scenario di lavoro, non il programma verificato.
