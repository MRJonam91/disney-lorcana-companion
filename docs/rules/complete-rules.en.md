# Disney Lorcana Official Rules & Tournament Guide (EN)

Comprehensive rulebook covering turn structure, timing, inking mechanics, and combat.

---

## 1. Win Condition
The first Illumineer to collect **20 Lore** instantly wins the game.

---

## 2. Deckbuilding Restrictions
- Minimum **60 cards** in deck.
- Up to **2 ink colors** maximum.
- Up to **4 copies** of any single card (by exact title and subtitle).

---

## 3. Turn Anatomy

### Beginning Phase (Ready, Set, Draw)
1. **Ready**: Turn all exerted cards upright into the ready position.
2. **Set**: Check and resolve any start-of-turn passive abilities (e.g. Location passive lore).
3. **Draw**: Draw the top card of your deck. *(First player skips draw step on turn 1).*

### Main Phase (In Any Order)
- **Ink**: Place 1 inkable card face-down into your inkwell (once per turn).
- **Play a Card**: Exert ink equal to the card's cost to summon it.
- **Quest**: Exert a dry character to gain lore equal to its lore value.
- **Challenge**: Exert a dry character to attack an **exerted** opposing character. Both deal damage simultaneously.
- **Sing Songs**: Exert a character of equal or greater cost to play an Action - Song for free.
- **Move to Location**: Pay the location's move cost to station a character there.
- **Use Abilities**: Pay costs to trigger item or character activated effects.

---

## 4. Persistent Damage
Damage marked on characters stays between turns. When marked damage equals or exceeds Willpower, the character is banished to the discard pile.
