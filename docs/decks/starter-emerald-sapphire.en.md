# Disney Lorcana Decks & Meta Guide (EN)

Complete 60-card starter list and competitive tier breakdown.

---

## 1. Recommended Starter Deck: Emerald / Sapphire "Mickey & Minnie Duo Shift"
- **Format**: Standard 60 Cards
- **Playstyle**: Synergy Aggro / Midrange
- **Inkable Ratio**: 54 Inkables / 6 Non-Inkables (Maximum consistency)

### Core Combo: Duo Shift
- **Turn 1**: Play 1-cost *Minnie Mouse*.
- **Turn 2**: Play 2-cost *Mickey Mouse*.
- **Turn 3**: Pay **Duo Shift 0** to fuse them into *Mickey & Minnie - Adventuring Duo* (5/5 with **5 Lore**!).
